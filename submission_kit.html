Final Submission Kit

Everything you need to successfully package your UI/UX design internship project, host it live, and publish your LinkedIn showcase video.

🌐 1. Deploying Live & GitHub Setup

To get a public link to share on the final submission form, host your files online for free.

1

Push to GitHub

Create a public repository named uiux_project on your GitHub account, drag and drop your workspace files (index, levels, style.css) into it, and commit them.

2

Deploy Live (Free)

Go to Netlify.com or Vercel.com. Drag and drop your project folder directly into their upload panel. It will give you a live https://... site link instantly!

3

Note Down Your Links

Save both the **GitHub Repository URL** and the **Netlify Live Demo URL** in a safe place. You will need to enter them in the Cognifyz submission form.

📹 2. Showcase Video Outline

Use a screen recorder (like OBS, Loom, or Windows Xbox Game Bar: Win + Alt + R) to make a 1-2 minute walkthrough video. 

[0:00 - 0:15] Hub Overview:
Start on index.html. Introduce yourself: "Hi, I am presenting my final UI/UX internship submission showcase..."

[0:15 - 0:35] Level 1 & 2:
Go to Level 1. Click a few color cards in the Color Simulator to show colors shifting on the mobile phone mockup. 
Go to Level 2. Show the Aura speaker layout, and click play on Sunny and Rainy SVG weather icons.

[0:35 - 1:00] Level 3 & 4:
Go to Level 3. Type an email and a weak password to show the strength meter, toggle show/hide. Hover navigation items.
Go to Level 4. Click a mood board image to show the design rationale panel. Then click the Checkout button to trigger the loader-to-success confetti burst!

[1:00 - 1:15] Wrap up:
Show the dashboard hub once more and thank the reviewers.
